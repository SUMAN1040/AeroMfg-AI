Hardware Engineering & RF Propagation

Zero-Interference RF Architecture in Metal-Dense Hangars

Aerospace manufacturing plants present severe multipath reflection and RF shadowing due to aluminum airframe skins and steel crane gantries. AeroMfg AI overcomes these constraints using adaptive antenna polarization, MIMO beamforming, and spread-spectrum modulation.

◈ Ceramic On-Metal UHF RFID

Utilizes engineered dielectric matching layers that tune tag impedance directly against conductive aerospace alloys. Operates reliably across 860-960 MHz with a read range up to 12 meters through composite radomes.

  • Withstands 260°C autoclave cycle at 7 bar pressure
  • IP69K high-pressure steam and washdown rated
  • Zero outgassing compliant with NASA SP-R-0022A
  • Laser-engraved ATA Spec 2000 2D DataMatrix code

◈ Sub-GHz & UWB Anchor Topology

Ceiling-mounted anchor arrays utilize Power over Ethernet (PoE 802.3at) to synchronize nanosecond clock pulses over PTP IEEE 1588. Transmits on UWB Channel 5 (6.489 GHz) and Channel 9 (7.987 GHz) to maintain zero cross-talk with avionics.

  • 16-element circular antenna array for 3D elevation tracking
  • Dynamic transmit power control (-41.3 dBm/MHz FCC mask)
  • Automated multipath reflection filtering via Kalman filter
  • Intrinsic safety ATEX Zone 1 / Class I Div 1 approved

◈ Cleanroom Environmental Pods

Continuous optical particle counters detecting 0.3 µm and 0.5 µm particulates in real time. Sends instantaneous telemetry to plant HVAC automation if airlock doors open excessively or filter pressure drops.

  • Optical light-scattering particle counter (0.1 CFM flow)
  • Static dissipative PVDF housing prevents ESD buildup
  • Battery backup with 48-hour continuous run autonomy
  • MQTT / RESTful interface to building management systems
