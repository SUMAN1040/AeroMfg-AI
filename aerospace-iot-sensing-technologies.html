AeroMfg AI Engineering Solution: Retrofitted legacy fluid-control rigs and EGSE with industrial telemetry modules, streaming real-time pressure, flow rate, and vibration diagnostics to an AI anomaly dashboard with automated threshold alerting.
Verified Operational Results: Launch-day hold events related to ground infrastructure failures were reduced to zero across consecutive mission cycles.
Strategic Takeaway: Real-time diagnostics of ground-side propellant hardware are vital for reducing launch-day risk.

◈ Global Supply Chain Mapping: Virginia

Operational Challenge: An aerospace integrator in Arlington struggled to manage the global distribution of flight-critical sub-assemblies across three continents, causing confusion about current hardware locations.
AeroMfg AI Engineering Solution: Deployed a global IoT-based tracking system utilizing satellite-telemetry sensors on all large shipping crates, integrated with an AI-augmented logistics dashboard that predicts arrival times and updates local integration teams automatically.
Verified Operational Results: Transit-related hardware loss events were completely mitigated, and payload delivery predictability increased by 55%.
Strategic Takeaway: Real-time status visibility across international boundaries is critical for multi-site integration efforts.

◈ High-Density Metal Tagging: Montreal, Canada

Operational Challenge: A satellite propulsion developer in Montreal experienced signal attenuation and reading errors when using standard RFID tags on high-density metallic thruster housings.
AeroMfg AI Engineering Solution: Retrofitted the thruster assembly line with ceramic, on-metal UHF RFID tags and high-sensitivity reader arrays, calibrating reader power levels to eliminate RF reflections within metallic assembly bays.
Verified Operational Results: Scanning accuracy reached 100%, eliminating manual tag verification steps and saving 120 technician hours per build cycle.
Strategic Takeaway: Specialized ceramic substrate tags are essential for reliable telemetry on metal flight components.
