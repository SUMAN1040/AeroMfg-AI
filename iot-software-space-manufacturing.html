Software Architecture & Data Fabric

Immutable As-Built Pedigree & Shop Floor Orchestration

Modern commercial airframes and satellite constellations require thousands of coordinated subassemblies with strict zero-defect tolerances. AeroMfg AI delivers an event-driven microservices architecture optimized for aerospace telemetry ingestion and audit compliance.

◈ Cryptographic Merkle Tree Genealogy

Every serialized component undergoes cryptographic hashing at each assembly event. Torque logs, thermal exposure data, and laser tracker coordinates are appended to an immutable Merkle DAG, ensuring non-repudiation across the 30-year operational lifecycle of the airframe.

  • SHA-256 state hashing on every fastener fastening event
  • Instant provenance lookup by aircraft tail or serial number
  • Tamper-evident logs verifiable by FAA and DCMA auditors
  • Full compliance with ATA Spec 2000 Chapter 9 requirements

◈ Real-Time Takt Time Optimization

Calculates workstation flow efficiency in real time. If Station 42 (Wing-to-Body Splice) encounters an out-of-spec shimming delay, the software resequences upcoming AGV deliveries and reprioritizes technician tasks across adjacent bays.

  • Automated andon alert escalation based on dwell thresholds
  • Predictive buffer sizing for long-lead titanium fasteners
  • Dynamic work instruction dispatch to technician tablets
  • Reduces assembly cycle times by an average of 18.4%

◈ Automated Quality Release Engine

Eliminates paper-based traveler sign-offs. Station completion requires positive wireless sign-offs from calibrated smart tooling, optical inspection cameras, and certified technician badge telemetry before proceeding to the next gate.

  • Automated FAA Form 8130-3 and EASA Form 1 package compilation
  • Zero undocumented scrap or uninspected part progression
  • Real-time statistical process control (SPC) charting
  • Bi-directional sync with Siemens Opcenter & Dassault DELMIA
