Satellite Integration & Assembly Intelligence (AI & T)

Satellite Assembly, Integration, and Testing (AI & T) demands extreme precision and relentless monitoring of critical path components. AeroMfg AI streamlines satellite bus fabrication and payload integration by tracking the exact movement, location, and handling of flight-certified sub-assemblies.

◈ Sub-Meter 3D Location Tracking

Sub-meter 3D location tracking for satellite structural panels, solar array wings, and propulsion tanks within high-bay integration complexes, preventing physical bottlenecks during multi-payload campaigns.

◈ Calibrated Torque & Fastener Verification

Automated tool tracking and calibrated torque wrench integration during optical payload mounting, verifying that every fastener meets exact engineering torque and thread-locking specifications.

◈ Vibration & TVAC Shock Telemetry

Real-time vibration and shock telemetry during acoustic and thermal vacuum (TVAC) chamber transport, capturing structural acceleration data to prevent latent mechanical defects.

◈ Digital Thread Genealogy Mapping

Digital thread genealogy mapping links every flight component serial number with cleanroom technician badges, environmental exposure logs, and non-conformance reports (NCRs).

◈ Automated FOD Prevention Protocols

Automated Foreign Object Debris (FOD) prevention protocols generate instant alerts if un-inventoried assembly tools remain inside fairing encapsulation zones before launch vehicle mating.

◈ Predictive Assembly Cadence Analytics

Predictive assembly timeline analytics cross-reference active work-in-progress (WIP) status against target launch windows to optimize resource allocation across integration shifts.

Launch Pad GSE & Ground Control Infrastructure Scenarios

Ground Support Equipment (GSE) at launch pads and launch vehicle integration facilities requires continuous operational verification to support aggressive launch manifests. AeroMfg AI monitors the location, maintenance status, and operational readiness of critical ground support hardware.

◈ Mobile Launch Platform Tracking

Automated tracking of mobile launch platforms, umbilical arms, and transporter-erectors across launch complex perimeters using ruggedized, weatherproof AIoT sensors.

◈ Cryogenic Flow & Line Pressure Monitoring

Real-time pressure, temperature, and cryogenic flow monitoring across liquid oxygen (LOX) and liquid methane/hydrogen fueling lines during launch countdown dress rehearsals.

◈ GSE Calibration Expiration Safeguards

GSE calibration expiration alerts prevent un-certified umbilical quick-disconnect fittings or pneumatic testing consoles from being deployed on active launch pads.

◈ Hazardous Exclusion Zone Verification

Hazardous exclusion zone access logging verifies that all pad personnel clear the strongback and pad apron before propellant loading sequence initiation.

◈ Post-Launch Blast Damage Telemetry

Post-launch blast damage assessment telemetry monitors pad structural strain, flame deflector thermal shock, and suppression water line pressure integrity.

◈ Flight Director C2 Software Integration

Integration with launch control C2 software provides automated GSE readiness checklists directly to Flight Directors during Go/No-Go polls.

Cleanroom Traceability & Environmental Compliance AI

Cleanroom environments (ISO Class 5, 7, and 8) require continuous environmental verification to prevent contamination of optical mirrors, sensitive sensors, and flight electronics. AeroMfg AI fuses sensor telemetry with spatial tracking to automate contamination control.

◈ Spatial Airborne Particulate Sensing

Continuous airborne particulate count, relative humidity, and ambient temperature monitoring cross-referenced with exact asset location coordinates.

◈ VOC & Outgassing Detection Arrays

Outgassing and volatile organic compound (VOC) sensor arrays surrounding optical payload integration bays trigger automated HVAC air scrubbing adjustments.

◈ ESD Potential Flooring & Strap Grounding

Electrostatic discharge (ESD) potential monitoring on technician grounding wrist straps and conductive flooring mats near sensitive CMOS satellite sensors.

◈ Automated Airlock Pass-Through Logs

Automated cleanroom airlock pass-through tracking ensures that raw materials and tools undergo full particulate blow-down before entering high-purity zones.

◈ Immutable Compliance Exposure Certs

Historical environmental exposure logs generate immutable compliance certificates required for NASA and Department of Defense flight hardware acceptance reviews.

◈ Differential Cleanroom Pressure Audits

Differential pressure monitoring between adjacent cleanrooms prevents reverse-flow dust intrusion during large payload door openings.

◈ Automated Receiving & CoC Reconciliation

◈ Fastener & Titanium Stock Forecasting

◈ Anti-Counterfeit Hardware Ledger

◈ Depot Overhaul Operational Dashboards
