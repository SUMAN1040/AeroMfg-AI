Application Overview for Commercial Aircraft Manufacturing

AeroMfg AI provides mission-critical operational intelligence tailored for the high consequence environments inherent in Commercial Aircraft Manufacturing, orbital hardware integration, and launch operations. By deploying advanced AIoT architectures, we enable real-time visibility into high-value flight hardware, contamination-controlled cleanroom environments, and complex ground support infrastructure. These applications are engineered to ensure mission success, eliminate foreign object debris (FOD) risks, and enforce strict configuration management throughout the satellite and space vehicle lifecycle.

High-Value Flight Hardware Visibility

Continuous sub-meter spatial tracking across large aerospace high bays, monitoring structural panels, fairings, and optical components without multipath distortion.

Cleanroom Enclosure Governance

Automated particulate counter integration, airlock pass-through enforcement, and differential pressure validation for ISO Class 5 to 8 integration zones.

FOD Elimination & Configuration Control

Digital tool interlocks, calibrated torque logging, and serial genealogy verification preventing unverified hardware from entering vehicle encapsulation envelopes.

Satellite Integration & Assembly Intelligence (AI & T)

◈ Sub-Meter 3D Location Tracking

Sub-meter 3D location tracking for satellite structural panels, solar array wings, and propulsion tanks within high-bay integration complexes, preventing physical bottlenecks during multi-payload campaigns.

◈ Calibrated Torque & Fastener Verification

Automated tool tracking and calibrated torque wrench integration during optical payload mounting, verifying that every fastener meets exact engineering torque and thread-locking specifications.

◈ Vibration & TVAC Shock Telemetry

Real-time vibration and shock telemetry during acoustic and thermal vacuum (TVAC) chamber transport, capturing structural acceleration data to prevent latent mechanical defects.

◈ Digital Thread Genealogy Mapping

Digital thread genealogy mapping links every flight component serial number with cleanroom technician badges, environmental exposure logs, and non-conformance reports (NCRs).

◈ Automated FOD Prevention Protocols

Automated Foreign Object Debris (FOD) prevention protocols generate instant alerts if un-inventoried assembly tools remain inside fairing encapsulation zones before launch vehicle mating.

◈ Predictive Assembly Cadence Analytics

Predictive assembly timeline analytics cross-reference active work-in-progress (WIP) status against target launch windows to optimize resource allocation across integration shifts.

Launch Pad GSE & Ground Control Infrastructure Scenarios

◈ Mobile Launch Platform Tracking

Automated tracking of mobile launch platforms, umbilical arms, and transporter-erectors across launch complex perimeters using ruggedized, weatherproof AIoT sensors.

◈ Cryogenic Flow & Line Pressure Monitoring

Real-time pressure, temperature, and cryogenic flow monitoring across liquid oxygen (LOX) and liquid methane/hydrogen fueling lines during launch countdown dress rehearsals.

◈ GSE Calibration Expiration Safeguards

GSE calibration expiration alerts prevent un-certified umbilical quick-disconnect fittings or pneumatic testing consoles from being deployed on active launch pads.

◈ Hazardous Exclusion Zone Verification

Hazardous exclusion zone access logging verifies that all pad personnel clear the strongback and pad apron before propellant loading sequence initiation.

◈ Flight Director C2 Software Integration

◈ ESD Potential Flooring & Strap Grounding
