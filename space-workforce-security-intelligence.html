Systems Architecture & Logic

Cognitive Spatial Geofencing & Zero-Trust Worker Protection

In high-stakes commercial aerospace manufacturing and launch vehicle integration facilities, traditional safety tethers and passive perimeter fences introduce severe operational bottlenecks. AeroMfg AI deploys an active, multi-layer AIoT spatial matrix that synchronizes real-time personnel positioning with automated machinery control loops.

◈ Sub-Meter Proximity Geofencing

Utilizes dual-band IEEE 802.15.4z UWB wearable smart badges transmitting time-difference-of-arrival (TDoA) pulses to high-bay ceiling anchors. Edge compute gateways calculate 3D Cartesian coordinates \((x, y, z)\) at 50 updates per second per technician.

  • Dynamic 2.5m warning buffer around active robotic drill units
  • Sub-15ms e-stop signal injection over safety-rated industrial Ethernet
  • Multipath reflection cancellation in metal airframe bays
  • Rechargeable LiPo badge with 30-day shift autonomy

◈ Multi-Factor Perimeter Verification

Replaces static badge swipes with continuous proximity authentication. As a technician approaches an ISO Class 5 payload integration cleanroom or ITAR-restricted avionics cell, the system cross-references active certifications in real-time.

  • Automated verification of AS9100 Rev D torque & solder certifications
  • Real-time ESD wrist strap resistance & grounding verification
  • Instant lockdown if unbadged visitors breach safety rings
  • Full cryptographic logging matching NIST SP 800-161 guidelines

◈ Fatigue & Safety Pattern Recognition

Recurrent neural networks (RNN) continuously evaluate workforce velocity profiles, micro-pauses, and spatial heatmaps across 12-hour launch countdown and wing-splice shifts to detect cognitive fatigue and bottleneck formation.

  • Early warning alerts for repetitive strain & unsafe posture patterns
  • Automated balance load redistribution for station leads
  • Zero personally identifiable biometric retention (privacy compliant)
  • Direct integration into SAP S/4HANA & Dassault DELMIA MES
