Zero-Defect GSE Governance & Cleanroom Asset Telemetry

Manufacturing flight-rated orbital systems requires flawless tool calibration and continuous environmental custody. An uncalibrated torque wrench or an unverified ground hoist introduces catastrophic failure modes during aerodynamic and orbital stress events. AeroMfg AI provides automated physical-to-digital synchronization.

◈ Predictive Calibration Lifecycle

Calculates usage degradation curves based on torque cycle counts, thermal exposures, and mechanical duty cycles. Alerts metrology teams 14 days prior to ISO 17025 expiration and disables smart station power if uncalibrated tools enter high-bays.

  • Automated smart tool disabling via wireless interlocks
  • NIST-traceable calibration certificate digitization
  • Complete cycle counting and strain-gauge telemetry
  • Eliminates 100% of out-of-calibration assembly events

◈ Transit & Cleanroom Custody

Ruggedized IoT sensor tags measure 3-axis shock, rotational vibration, relative humidity, and temperature during inter-facility highway transit and high-bay crane transport.

  • Tri-axial shock logging up to 500g with 0.1g resolution
  • Autonomous alerts if vibration limits exceed MIL-STD-810H
  • Hermetic IP68 enclosures with 5-year battery lifetimes
  • Zero cleanroom outgassing compliant with NASA SP-R-0022A

◈ FAL Kitting & Takt Synchronization

Synchronizes staging carts with ERP production schedules. If a critical component—such as an avionics flight computer or hydrazine manifold—fails an incoming quality gate, downstream assembly stages are dynamically rescheduled.

  • Real-time bill-of-materials (BOM) discrepancy detection
  • Automated pick-and-pack verification with smart light tags
  • Reduces search waste from 45 min/shift to under 90 seconds
  • Full integration into SAP S/4HANA & Siemens Teamcenter
