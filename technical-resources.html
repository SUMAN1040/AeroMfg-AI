Application Overview for Commercial Aircraft Manufacturing

AeroMfg AI delivers industrial-grade operational intelligence engineered specifically for the stringent tolerances of aerospace assembly and orbital hardware integration. Designed to operate reliably inside metallic, RF-challenging high bays and ISO-classified cleanrooms, our platform connects edge sensor telemetry directly to enterprise MES and PLM layers. By synchronizing tool calibration data, monitoring atmospheric thresholds, and maintaining automated component genealogy, engineering teams eliminate foreign object debris risks and maintain complete digital thread auditability throughout aircraft assembly cycles.

Flight Hardware Traceability

Real-time spatial tracking across large aerospace high bays, monitoring wing assemblies, fuselage sections, and sensitive avionics without multipath RF distortion.

Cleanroom Environmental Control

Automated particulate counter integration, airlock pass-through logging, and differential pressure validation for ISO Class 5 through Class 8 integration cleanrooms.

Regulatory Configuration Assurance

Automated metrology tracking, calibrated torque cycle counting, and digital certificate mapping meeting AS9100 Rev D, ITAR, and FAA Part 21 requirements.
