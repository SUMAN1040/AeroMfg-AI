Frequently Asked Technical & Compliance Questions

Q1: Does UWB or BLE radio frequency energy interfere with live aircraft avionics testing?

No. AeroMfg AI RF hardware strictly complies with RTCA DO-160G Section 21 Category M and MIL-STD-461G. Our transmitters operate within specific ultra-wideband spectral masks (-41.3 dBm/MHz) well outside safety-critical avionics navigation, VHF communication, and ILS localizer frequency allocations.

Q2: How does the system handle employee biometric privacy in workforce tracking?

AeroMfg AI uses zero biometric data storage. Wearable smart badges utilize anonymized cryptographic identifiers (e.g. `BADGE-0842`) that map strictly to shift certifications and training qualifications. No facial recognition, heart rate, or personal biometric variables are captured or stored, ensuring 100% compliance with GDPR, California CPRA, and union collective bargaining agreements.

Q3: What is the fail-safe behavior if an edge gateway loses network connectivity?

Every local edge compute gateway operates in an autonomous fail-safe mode. Local safety geofences and machinery emergency-stop relays continue functioning locally with zero cloud dependence. Telemetry events are cached in high-speed non-volatile memory (up to 72 hours of offline storage) and automatically synchronized with the central cloud upon link restoration.

Q4: Can AeroMfg AI integrate with existing MES systems like Dassault DELMIA or SAP ME?

Yes. AeroMfg AI provides certified pre-built connectors for SAP S/4HANA Manufacturing, Dassault Systèmes DELMIA Apriso, Siemens Opcenter, and PTC Windchill. Integration is established over OPC-UA, MQTT 5.0, and secure RESTful Webhooks, eliminating custom middleware requirements.

🛡 NIST Cybersecurity Framework & SOC 2 Type II Certified

AeroMfg AI platform infrastructure is audited under SOC 2 Type II, ISO/IEC 27001, and NIST SP 800-171 standards for Controlled Unclassified Information (CUI). All communication links utilize TLS 1.3 with mutual certificate authentication (mTLS) and FIPS 140-3 validated cryptographic modules.
